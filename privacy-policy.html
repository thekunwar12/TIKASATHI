TIKA SAATHI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Overview

TIKA SAATHI is a vaccine decision support tool designed for frontline health workers (ANM and ASHA) under India's Universal Immunisation Programme (UIP). This privacy policy explains how we handle information in the app.

Data Collection

TIKA SAATHI does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

The app operates entirely offline on the user's device. No information entered by the user (such as date of birth, vaccination history, or any other input) is stored, saved, or transmitted to any server, database, or third party.

No User Accounts

The app does not require user registration, login, or any form of authentication. There are no user accounts.

No Analytics or Tracking

The app does not use any analytics tools, cookies, tracking pixels, advertising identifiers, or any other mechanism to monitor or track user behavior.

No Internet Required

TIKA SAATHI works 100% offline after installation. It does not make any network requests or connect to any external servers during use.

No Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate with any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs that collect user data.

Data Entered by Users

Any data entered by the user (such as a child's date of birth or previously given vaccines) is used solely for real-time calculation within the app session. This data:

  • Is processed entirely on the user's device
  • Is not stored or saved anywhere
  • Is lost when the app is closed or refreshed
  • Is never transmitted to any server

WhatsApp Sharing

The app includes an optional "Share via WhatsApp" feature. When used, it generates a text summary of the vaccination schedule and opens WhatsApp for the user to send it. The content is created locally on the device and shared only at the user's discretion. TIKA SAATHI does not access WhatsApp contacts or messages.

Children's Privacy

While the app is used in the context of child immunisation, it does not collect or store any personally identifiable information about children. The date of birth entered is used only for real-time age calculation and is not retained.

Permissions

TIKA SAATHI does not request any device permissions (camera, contacts, location, storage, microphone, etc.).

Changes to This Policy

If we make any changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Since the app collects no data, significant changes are unlikely.
